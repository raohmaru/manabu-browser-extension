# Architecture Decision Records

Record important architecture decisions here when they are made. Include the context,
the decision, and its consequences. If a decision changes, add a new dated record instead
of replacing the earlier one.

## YYYY-MM-DD: Decision title

- **Context:** What problem or constraints prompted this decision?
- **Decision:** What was chosen?
- **Consequences:** What are the trade-offs, benefits, and follow-up implications?
